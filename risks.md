# Risks

**If you sell.** You give up upside above the strike (calls), or you may have to buy tokens that keep falling (puts).
The premium is yours regardless.

**If you buy.** You can lose the whole premium if the option is never worth exercising.

**Timing.** Exercise needs a confirmed transaction before the expiry timestamp. Do not wait for the last minute.

**Token risk.** Listed tokens can be volatile, illiquid, or carry issuer controls such as blocklists and pauses.

**Smart contract risk.** The contracts are tested, but any contract can have bugs. Only use funds you can afford to
lose. Report problems to contact@kyte.sh.

Nothing here is investment advice. Not available where prohibited. Kyte is built on Robinhood Chain and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Robinhood.
